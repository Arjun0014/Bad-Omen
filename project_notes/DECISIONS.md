# BAD OMEN — Design & Technical Decisions

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Vite + plain ES modules + three.js 0.186 + @dimforge/rapier3d-compat 0.21** | Fastest iteration; no TS compile friction under jam time; Rapier compat inlines WASM (no extra asset paths). |
| 2 | **Komur from the supplied Somali cat .blend**, recoloured by rasterising a per-vertex marking mask into a new 1K texture | Keeps the original rig/mesh/animations (CC-BY, credited) while delivering the required black coat + white paws/chest/tail tip + amber eyes. |
| 3 | **Procedural cat actions authored on the existing IK rig** (foot/paw IK targets + spine/head/tail rotations), exported with glTF sampling which bakes the IK | No human retargeting onto a quadruped; gaits (trot/gallop/sneak) generated from phase-offset footfall patterns; verified with contact sheets from the re-imported GLB. |
| 4 | **Grandma = Mixamo goblin + 27 Mixamo clips** combined in Blender; root motion stripped (trend or full) so AI drives position; release frames measured from hand speed | AI-controlled kinematic capsule; projectiles leave the hand on the measured release frame. |
| 5 | **Witch hat, spectacles, broom added in-engine** to Grandma's bones | Instantly reads as a witchy grandma without touching the licensed mesh. |
| 6 | **House built procedurally in code** (merged static meshes per material + box colliders) with Poly Haven wood PBR (ARM maps feed AO/roughness/metal directly) | Reliable, fast to iterate, deterministic colliders; Blender reserved for characters. |
| 7 | **Props are procedural part-built prefabs**; fracture turns parts into physical debris that later freezes into persistent static meshes | Every object breaks into recognisable pieces, damage persists, physics cost bounded (≤90 active debris bodies). |
| 8 | **Impact detection by per-step Δv** on dynamic breakables + contact-force events for fixtures | Robust "it fell off the table and shattered" without tuning collider force events per prop. |
| 9 | **Provenance**: every breakable remembers its last toucher (sticky Grandma source); breaks inherit it | "GRANNY DID IT" x2 bonus applies to chains Grandma started (e.g. plates on a table she smashed). |
| 10 | **Weak points + bait aiming**: when Komur stands near a glowing weak point, Grandma's throw is aimed through him at it; if he fails to dodge he's bowled over and the throw is spent | Makes the core skill (bait + dodge) reliable and readable instead of depending on physics luck (docs/05). |
| 11 | **Set pieces = authored sequences** with skip-safe steps, slow-mo, camera shots, persistent aftermath; triggered only by real conditions (priming + Grandma impacts) | Spectacle without full structural simulation; never soft-locks; skip fast-forwards to the same end state. |
| 12 | **Railings use their own collision group** (block bodies + projectiles, not sight/camera) and are included in the nav line checks | Grandma can see Komur on the gallery; nav never string-pulls through rails. |
| 13 | **Mid-stairs path start = the stair end toward the goal floor**, no string-pulling on the flight | Fixed Grandma oscillating at the bottom of the stairs. |
| 14 | **Finale** unlocks at ~55% doom + 2 disasters + rage ≥75 (relaxes after 6–9 min); the enchanted axe homes in on the roof brace once Komur dodges | Guarantees a finale for any player who engages, still requires the bait/dodge. |
| 15 | **Audio**: ElevenLabs used only at build time (Node script reads .env); 35 lines with `eleven_v4`; most SFX + music synthesised procedurally in WebAudio | Starter account had ~2.6k credits; procedural audio keeps the bundle small and never blocks the build. |
| 16 | **Voices**: Grandma = account's "Cthulu female - v4 narrator" clone (picked over a "breathy/sensual" library voice), Komur = premade "George" (warm, dry British) | Character fit; Komur's lines are inner monologue so no lip-sync needed. |
| 17 | **Restart rebuilds the whole world** (new Scene + Rapier world, SkeletonUtils clones) | Guaranteed clean reset of physics, debris, fire and set-piece state. |
| 18 | **Tests drive the system Edge via Playwright `channel: 'msedge'`** | Playwright's bundled browser revision wasn't installed; system Edge has GPU WebGL. |
| 19 | Raw asset packs (`assets_inbox/**`) are git-ignored | Licensed source files must not be redistributed via the repo; only derived GLBs ship. |
| 20 | **Cat moves at gait speeds with momentum** (trot 2.6, gallop 5.1 m/s; velocity steers, drifts on hard reversals) | User playtest: old 4.6/7.2 m/s instant movement felt "gimmicky" and made the house feel small. |
| 21 | **Aimed leaps instead of a physics hop**: Space plans a leap to the surface ahead (prep → ballistic arc via the KCC → forepaws-first landing; >1.3 m rise = climb), an amber ring shows the target; free hop only when nothing is ahead | Real cats gauge and commit to jumps; makes counters/tables/beds feel like a cat climbing, not a block bouncing. |
| 22 | **Clips + procedural layer**: baked Blender clips for gaits/actions, then additive bone rotations at runtime (spine bend, tail lag, head look-at, slope pitch) | Whole-body reactivity without authoring every blend; gait playback = speed / measured clip speed (no foot slide). |
| 23 | **Grounded KCC moves are flat or slope-tangent, never into the ground** (cat and Grandma) | Rapier 0.21 KCC intermittently returns ~0 movement when the request points into the floor (measured ~7% of steps). |
| 24 | **Camera: constant distance, collides with architecture only, fades occluding furniture** | User: the camera "switching between away and close" caused headaches. |
| 25 | **House grows by a wing, not by rescaling** (conservatory/library + windows + wider yard) | Rescaling every coordinate would break all set pieces and wall-adjacent furniture; a wing adds space and ~150 breakables safely. |
| 26 | **Low quality keeps the post composer** (only bloom/MSAA off); shaders pre-compiled for the composer target | Rendering straight to the canvas changes every material's shader variant → multi-second recompile freeze when switching. |
| 27 | **Front-door cat flap** (CATONLY blocker stops Grandma) | Breakable windows let the cat reach the porch; the flap guarantees it can always get back in. |
| 28 | **LightRig: a fixed pool of 12 real point lights fed by virtual lights** (house, candles, fires, chandeliers, brace, enchanted axe), picked per frame by relevance to the cat and camera, fading in and out | Hiding a broken candle's light changed the light count and recompiled every shader: a 5.2 s freeze when Grandma smashed the dining table (user playtest 2). Also halves per-pixel light cost (24 → 13). |
| 29 | **Break fragments and particles spawn on a ~4 ms per-frame budget** (the prop stays visible, frozen, until its own fragments appear); score/noise/set-piece effects stay immediate | A 13-prop chain built ~50 meshes and bodies in one frame (60 ms of JS). |
| 30 | **Banner/objective pops use the Web Animations API** | The remove-class/offsetWidth/add-class restart forced a synchronous layout, ~8 ms per banner. |
| 31 | **Moon shadows only from props and debris where moonlight reaches** (yard, glass-roofed wing) | The roof keeps moonlight out of the house: ~800 shadow-pass draw calls for nothing (1482 → 1023 calls per frame). |
| 32 | **Default gait = a long-stride procedural catwalk at 1.45 m/s; Shift = gallop at 3.9 m/s** (was trot 2.6 / gallop 5.1); Grandma's chase 2.05–3.2 m/s | Playtest 2: the run felt weird and too fast. The old clips' short strides played at ~3× (scurrying); the user asked for a slower, graceful walk. A walking cat can be caught, a galloping one never. |
| 33 | **Idle life**: shuffled fidgets every ~3–5 s (look around, sniff, groom, stretch, tail chase, nudging a knick-knack off the furniture), sit at 18 s, lie down at 30 s; 12 voiced idle quips | Playtest 2: "when idle it can do stuff and say things as well". Grandma hunting nearby keeps him alert instead. |
| 34 | **Dialogue rules**: the same line never twice within 5 s; non-priority `{ queue: true }` lines wait for the current speaker and expire after ~4.5 s; cutscene replies and rage/finale lines queue | Priority lines cut each other off mid-sentence; queued lines otherwise played scenes later, out of context. |
| 35 | **Banner priority** (disasters, finale, "SHE'S THE WEAPON" = 2; rage and combo = 1) | A MELTDOWN banner replaced "THE BEDROOM BELOW" while it was up. |
| 36 | **Camera pivot 0.72 m with 0.45 m look-ahead; tail trim 0.78; Komur's fill light fixed 1.3 m toward the lens** | The raised tail covered the view ahead; a fill light placed a fraction of the camera distance away blew his fur white in close-ups. |
| 37 | **Gameplay video recorded from the real game** (CDP screencast incl. HUD + in-page MediaRecorder on the WebAudio master, then ffmpeg cut lists) | Silent capture on a shared machine, real mechanics and voices, repeatable takes (~5 min per take). |

| 39 | **The tail droops (up to 0.9 rad) when the follow camera is pressed close behind Komur** (< 1.6 m, camera behind him) | Walls and corners pull the camera in; a raised tail then covers the view. Rises again as the camera backs off. |
| 40 | **Komur's fill light has no distance falloff inside a 2.4 m reach** (decay 0, 0.75) | With decay 1.6 a white cup or teapot near the lens got ~10x the light his fur gets and bloomed into a white blob (conservatory foreground); flat, it lights him the same. |
| 41 | **Moonlit prop parts smaller than ~12 cm cast no moon shadow** | Books, cups and pots: a speck of shadow each but a shadow-pass draw call apiece (362 → 290 casters). |
| 42 | **No prop-mesh merging** (the handoff's optional ≈300-draw-call idea) | Measured upper bounds on the iGPU: hiding every prop +2–9 fps, no shadow pass +2–7 fps. The game isn't draw-call bound; not worth a risky refactor of the fracture system before the deadline. |
| 43 | **`disposeWorld` frees per-world GPU resources** (cloned skeletons' bone textures, the moon's shadow map, per-world canvas textures) and keeps cached / GLB textures | Restarts leaked ~11 textures each (85 → 118 over 4 restarts); now flat. |
| 44 | **Skipping a cutscene silences its remaining lines** (step-level `skip` handlers, `Dialogue.hush()`) | `Sequence.skip()` runs every remaining step at once: the intro's lines, meow and door slam all fired together and the last line cut Grandma off.
