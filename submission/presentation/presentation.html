Game Jam Europe 2026 · starring Kömür the black cat

Who’s the real bad omen?

A superstitious witch-grandma is sure the black cat in her cottage is bad luck. In BAD OMEN she is right about the disasters and wrong about the culprit: Kömür never brings her house down. Every big disaster is thrown by her own hand.

1

The blame

A black cat strolls into her house, and Grandma already knows whose fault the next disaster will be. Every broken plate: “That cat!”

2

The bait

Kömür is just a cat. He can’t snap a rope or bring down a roof. He knocks the china about, meows, taunts and dodges, and waits for her temper to go.

3

The backfire

Her own pans, axes and pitchforks snap the chandelier rope, light the stove, crack the floor, shatter the glass roof and knock out the last roof brace.

The rules tell the joke: the house’s weak points break only under Grandma’s throws, and every disaster she causes scores GRANNY DID IT ×2. The more she believes he is bad luck, the worse her own luck gets.

How Kömür is in the game

He looks like Kömür

black coat, white paws, chest and tail tip, amber eyes: the base cat recoloured into the jam’s mascot, with 38 new animations.

He plays like a cat

You are Kömür: walk, gallop, drift round corners, leap onto tables and mantels, pounce, swipe, meow, hide, sneak and scratch.

He talks like one

A dry inner monologue in 49 voiced lines, against an increasingly unhinged Grandma (28 lines), all subtitled.

He lives like one

Leave him alone and he licks his paw, sits, settles into a loaf, or nudges something off the furniture.

How it plays · team Fractured Sanity

Don’t break the house. Make her break it.

Third person, in the browser: one cat, a two-storey wooden cottage, a glass-roofed conservatory and a walled garden, with about 375 breakable things that stay broken.

1

Make mischief

Swipe the good china off the sideboard, shred the curtains, set off chain reactions. Every crash raises her rage.

2

Bait her

Taunt her with a meow, then wait beside a glowing weak spot: a chandelier rope, the stove, the roof brace.

3

Dodge the throw

Every throw is telegraphed by a glowing arc. Jump clear at the last moment and her throw hits the weak spot instead.

4

The house pays

A cinematic cutaway, GRANNY DID IT ×2, and a house that stays wrecked. Her rage climbs, her throws get heavier.
